Model assumptions

Patched-conic, Sun-only two-body transfer; impulsive departure/arrival; zero-revolution (single-pass) Lambert; no perturbations or planetary gravity wells during flight. The transfer-cost proxy is v∞,dep + v∞,arr, not a modeled parking-orbit burn or launch-system delta-v. Plot cells are discrete samples; contours are visual guides.

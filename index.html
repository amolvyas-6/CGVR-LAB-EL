Model assumptions

Patched-conic, Sun-only two-body transfer; impulsive departure/arrival; zero-revolution (single-pass) Lambert; no perturbations or planetary gravity wells during flight. The chart ranks v∞,dep + v∞,arr. The selected transfer also shows an ideal propellant estimate for departure from Earth orbit and capture into destination orbit. Plot cells are discrete samples.
